Voice control

Tap the mic and speak, e.g. “open the gate”.
  • Lights: turn on the living room light, dim the bedroom to 30 percent, all lights off
  • Gate & fan: open the gate, stop the gate, fan speed 3, fan breeze, turn off the fan in 10 minutes
  • Security: arm security, disarm security, stop the alarm, is there a car in the garage
  • Strip & speaker: strip red, rainbow, party mode, volume up, ring the doorbell, good night
  • Alexa (after “Alexa, discover devices”): Alexa, turn on Gate, Alexa, set Fan to 100%, Alexa, set Strip Light to blue, Alexa, turn on Good Night
